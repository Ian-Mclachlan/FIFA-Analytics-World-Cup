# FWC26 Medical Surge — Probabilistic Forecasting Tools

Monte Carlo patient prediction tools for medical event-associated surge,
developed for the FIFA World Cup 2026 Medical Surge Operational Readiness Panel.

## Contents
- `FWC_2026.html` — Stadium-specific forecasting dashboard
  (FIFA CWC25 regression, calibrated to 63 matches)
- `surge-command.html` — Multi-model command-view forecasting tool
  (Arbon, FIFA, UVa, Baird/Perron stacked through Monte Carlo)

## Empirical anchors
Calibrated to FIFA Club World Cup 2025 data:
63 matches, 12 stadiums, mean PPR 0.31/1,000, mean TTHR 0.05/1,000.
Internal performance audit: R² = 0.24 (encounters), R² = 0.322 (transports).
